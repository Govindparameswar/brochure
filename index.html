What is Panchakarma?

The Ancient Science of Deep Healing

Panchakarma — meaning "Five Therapeutic Actions" — is Ayurveda's most powerful detoxification and rejuvenation system.

It cleanses the body at the cellular level, eliminates accumulated toxins (Ama), and restores the natural balance of the three doshas — Vata, Pitta, and Kapha. Every programme begins with a personalised physician consultation to understand your unique body constitution (Prakriti) and design the ideal treatment plan.

Purvakarma (Preparation Phase)

Snehana — Internal & external oleation with medicated herbal oils to loosen deep-seated toxins.
Swedana — Herbal steam therapy to open channels and mobilise toxins.

Pradhanakarma (Main Cleansing Phase)

The five core therapeutic actions (Shodanas) administered as per individual need to deeply purify the body.

Paschatkarma (Rejuvenation Phase)

Nourishing diet, herbal support, gentle routines & lifestyle guidance for long-term wellness.

Shodanas

The Five Core Therapies

These five therapies form the foundation of Pradhanakarma, providing deep cellular detoxification and doshic balance.

Vamana

Therapeutic emesis to expel excess Kapha — ideal for respiratory & skin conditions.

Virechana

Therapeutic purgation to eliminate excess Pitta — supports liver & digestive health.

Basti

Medicated enema — the most powerful Vata-balancing therapy; nourishes deep tissues.

Nasya

Nasal administration of herbal oils — clears sinus, improves brain & sensory function.

Raktamokshana

Blood purification therapy — eliminates toxins from the bloodstream.
